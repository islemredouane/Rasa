Student Life

Rasa is a cultural club founded in 2024 at the Higher School of Computer Sciences and Digital Technologies (ESTIN), Béjaïa. Created by a group of students, the club aims to ease academic pressure by fostering a vibrant cultural environment that brings together art, music, games, and creative expression. Through diverse activities and events, Rasa offers students a space to connect, relax, and express themselves beyond the classroom.

Frequently asked questions

What is Rasa Club?

add

Rasa is the cultural and artistic club of ESTIN (École Supérieure en Sciences et Technologies de l'Informatique et du Numérique) in Amizour, Béjaïa, Algeria. It was created by students to ease the pressure of studies with a space for literature, art, music, chess, games and cultural exchange. Its motto is “Feel the emotion. Live the expression.”

Where is Rasa Club located?

add

At ESTIN in Amizour, Béjaïa. The club room is on the ground floor of the ESTIN library, where many of its meetings and evenings take place.

What departments does Rasa have?

add

Six departments: Literature, Art, Music, Chess, Activities and Charity, supported by the Multimedia, Communication, Logistics and Human Resources teams.

Who leads Rasa Club in 2026/27?

add

The 2026/27 board is led by president Kernou Abdellah, with Zamoum Chemseddine as vice president and Slimani Sabrina as secretary. Meet the whole board.

What events does Rasa organise?

add

Each season includes a Welcome Day, movie nights, a trip to the Algiers International Book Fair (SILA), the 1 November commemoration, the Speaking Club in English and French, the Yennayer celebration, musical evenings, the RASA Chess Cup and outings such as Tikjda. The club ran 11 activities in 2025/26. See all events.

What is the RASA Chess Cup?

add

Rasa's chess tournament. The first edition took place at ESTIN from 15 to 17 February 2026 with 24 players; Sameur Adel won, ahead of Boukendoul Mahdi and Lahmar Mouaiz. Read the recap.

How can I join Rasa?

add

Follow @rasa.estin on Instagram for every call. For 2026/27 the club is recruiting graphic designers and an external relations member for its core team: apply here.

What does Rasa's Charity Department do?

add

It runs solidarity campaigns with accredited associations, starting with support for children with cancer, plus causes such as blood donation, Ramadan solidarity and winter clothing collections. Rasa never collects money online. Learn more.

How can I contact Rasa Club?

add

By email at rasa@estin.dz, on Instagram at @rasa.estin, or with the contact form on this website.
