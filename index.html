Student Life

Rasa is a cultural club founded in 2024 at the Higher School of Computer Sciences and Digital Technologies (ESTIN), Béjaïa. Created by a group of students, the club aims to ease academic pressure by fostering a vibrant cultural environment that brings together art, music, games, and creative expression. Through diverse activities and events, Rasa offers students a space to connect, relax, and express themselves beyond the classroom.
