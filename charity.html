Small hands, big hearts

We are students, not a charity organisation. That is exactly why we work hand in hand with those who are.

Solidarity Transparency Dignity

Our mission is to bring the energy of ESTIN students to the people who need it most in Béjaïa and beyond. Every RASA department can give something: an evening of music, a painting, a chess challenge, a video that moves people to act. We turn that into support for sick children, families in need and the causes that matter to our community.

Flagship campaign · in preparation

Hope for little fighters

Behind every hospital door in a paediatric oncology ward there is a child who would rather be drawing, playing or laughing. This season we want to raise funds and smiles for children fighting cancer, through a RASA solidarity evening and visits to children in treatment, together with an accredited cancer-support association.

  • music_noteA solidarity evening: music, art auction and chess challenges by RASA’s departments.
  • redeemGifts, books and art kits for children in treatment on special days.
  • medicationFunds for medicines and care, collected and spent by our partner association.

The causes we stand for

Six actions we want to carry through the year, each one close to the reality of Algerian families.

child_care

Children with cancer

Fundraising with a cancer-support association, gifts and creative workshops for children in paediatric oncology.

bloodtype

Blood donation

On-campus blood drives organised with the blood transfusion services. One donation can help several patients.

nightlight

Ramadan solidarity

Food baskets and iftar support for families in need, prepared with local associations.

ac_unit

Winter warmth

Collecting coats, blankets and shoes for families in the mountain villages of Béjaïa when the cold sets in.

backpack

Back to school

School bags and supplies for orphans and children from low-income families before the new school year.

campaign

Health awareness

Awareness actions such as Pink October for breast cancer screening, made with our Design and Multimedia teams.
