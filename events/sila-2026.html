Algiers International Book Fair (SILA 2026)

معرض الجزائر الدولي للكتاب — الدورة 29: «الغد بين دفتي كتاب»

Organized in partnership with Inmir Voyage agency, 48 Rasa Club members and ESTIN students from Béjaïa embarked on a day-long cultural journey to SAFEX Algiers. Together, we explored international pavilions, met publishers, celebrated student literature, and witnessed the launch of novel The White Keeper by our very own Nourhane Belgacem.
