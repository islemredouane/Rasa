Behind the Scenes

A Day Immersed in Literature

On Friday, 6 November 2026, the Rasa Club organized its flagship annual literary expedition to the 29th Algiers International Book Fair (SILA) held at SAFEX Pins Maritimes in Algiers, in official partnership with Inmir Voyage travel agency. Carrying this year's evocative banner «الغد بين دفتي كتاب» (Tomorrow between the covers of a book), the fair provided the ideal backdrop for a vibrant gathering of Algeria's youth, writers, and thinkers.

Departing early from the ESTIN campus in Amizour, Béjaïa aboard the bus arranged with Inmir Voyage (وكالة أنمير للسياحة والأسفار), forty-eight students and club members arrived in the capital united by their passion for storytelling, history, and creative expression. For many first-year students, it was their very first time discovering the sheer scale of SILA, navigating through the central halls, the pavilions of guest countries, and cultural forums.

"Seeing our own members publish novels while pursuing high-level computer science studies at ESTIN is the absolute embodiment of what Rasa stands for: bridging logic and emotion, coding and literature."

— Rasa Club Literature Team

The defining highlight of this year's edition was celebrating our club leader and talented author Nourhane Belgacem, who was present at the fair to introduce her novel The White Keeper (نزيل الغرفة البيضاء). Members rallied to support her at the publishing stands, exchanging copies, signing dedications, and engaging in discussions about the creative writing process.

From browsing rare editions at the المكتبة الخضراء stand to attending literary panels hosted by ANEP and the Ministry of Culture, the day was packed with discoveries. With seamless coordination from Inmir Voyage and our multimedia team armed with camera and gimbal stabilizer, every memory was documented—from morning laughs and book hauls to lively debates that carried all the way through the journey back to Amizour.
